# Lighter — Privacy Policy

Effective date: the day Lighter 1.0 is released on the App Store (draft last updated 19 November 2026)
App: Lighter for iPhone (bundle ID `com.vericodestudio.lighter`)
Brand: VeriCode — Independent Software Studio
Responsible party: VeriCode, operated by its proprietor in India ("we", "us")
Privacy contact: support@vericodestudio.com

## 1. Scope and summary
This policy applies to the Lighter app on iPhone and to the support emails you send us. Visits to this policy and to the VeriCode website, including links opened from the app, are described in section 8.

Lighter helps you review your photos and delete the ones you do not want.
- Lighter has no account, no server, no advertising and no analytics. It has no crash-reporting service.
- Your photos are never uploaded, copied or sent to us or to anyone else by the app. All processing happens on your iPhone.
- We do not track you and we do not share your data with third parties.

Local storage is still data handling, so this policy also says what the app keeps on your device and what controls you have.

## 2. What the app accesses
**Your photo library.** Lighter asks for permission to your Photos so it can show your photos and videos, let you mark them to keep or delete, and delete the ones you confirm. You can choose full access or a limited selection, and you can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Photos > Lighter. Lighter reads photos only to display and analyse them on your iPhone. It deletes only after you confirm in the standard iOS prompt.

**iCloud Photos.** If a photo or video is stored in iCloud Photos and not on your iPhone, iOS may download it from iCloud so Lighter can show or play it while you review it. That download is done by Apple's Photos service under your Apple Account. Lighter does not send the photo to us or to anyone else. The Quick clean-up scan uses only thumbnails already on your iPhone and skips photos that exist only in iCloud.

The app asks for no other permission. It does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, microphone, camera or calendar, and it does not use an advertising identifier or any account identifier.

## 3. What the app stores on your device
All of the following stays on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app. We cannot see it.

| Information | Why the app keeps it |
|---|---|
| Daily deletion count and date | To apply the free plan's limit of 50 deletions a day |
| Number of photos you have deleted and roughly how much space they took, added up since you installed Lighter | So Settings can show how much you have cleared |
| Your appearance choice (System, Light or Dark) | To remember your setting |
| Rating-prompt state, such as how many albums you finished and when you last saw the prompt | So the app does not ask for a rating too often |
| Quick clean-up scan results: for each analysed photo, its Photos-library identifier, its dates, whether it appears to show people or a document, a quality score and two numeric fingerprints | So the scan does not repeat on every launch, and so duplicates and "Pages & Things" can be listed |

The scan uses Apple's Vision framework on your iPhone, only while the app is open. The saved results are numbers and labels, not images. They are kept in the app's private storage and are never sent off your device.

Photos you have marked during a session are held only while you review them. If you close the app, the marks are lost and nothing is deleted.

## 4. How we use information
Only to run the app on your device. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, profile you or use it to train AI.

## 5. Purchases
Lighter offers an optional one-time in-app purchase, Lighter Pro. Apple handles the payment through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The app uses Apple's StoreKit to check whether Pro is unlocked, and "Restore Purchases" asks Apple to confirm an earlier purchase. Apple's own privacy policy governs that processing.

## 6. Storage and security
The app's data sits in storage that only the app can read, protected by the iOS app sandbox and your iPhone's passcode and encryption. The app adds no encryption or app lock of its own. Anyone who can unlock your iPhone and open Lighter can see your photos in the app, as they could in the Photos app.

## 7. Retention and deletion
- **Photos you delete** go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where they stay for 30 days and can be restored. With iCloud Photos turned on, deletions also apply to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.
- **Data the app stored** (section 3) is removed when you delete Lighter from your iPhone. Revoking photo access in iOS Settings stops the app from reading your photos.
- We keep no server-side copy of anything, so there is nothing for us to export or delete on your behalf.
- **Email.** If you email support@vericodestudio.com, we keep the correspondence for one year.

## 8. Service providers and other parties
The app contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting, payment or login provider and no third-party code that handles your data. Apple provides installation, updates, payments and iCloud under its own policies. If you email us, Gmail (Google) processes the message.

The Privacy Policy, Terms of Use and Support Page rows in Settings open our website in your browser. The Rate on the App Store row and the Contact Support row open the App Store and your email app. The app itself makes no network connections; these links leave the app only when you tap them. The legal pages are hosted through Cloudflare, with the rest of the VeriCode website. Visiting them in your browser sends technical request information, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested, to the host, as any website visit does. No photo or app data is included in those links.

## 9. Your choices and rights
You can use Lighter without giving us any information. You control what the app sees through iOS Settings > Photos, and what it keeps by deleting the app. Depending on where you live, the law may give you rights over personal data held about you, such as access, correction, deletion or objection. Because the app keeps its data only on your device, we cannot access those records. We can respond to requests about correspondence you send us and about website information, to the extent we hold it. Write to the privacy contact to make a request.

## 10. Children
Lighter is a general-audience utility. It is not directed at children under 13, it has no age check, and it collects no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 sent us personal information by email, write to the privacy contact and we will delete it.

## 11. International use
Your photos and the app's data stay on your iPhone. Apple, our email provider and our website host may process information in other countries. We operate from India.
