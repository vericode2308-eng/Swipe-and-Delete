# Lighter — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Lighter ("the app") is an iPhone app that helps you review your photos and delete the ones you don't want. It is published by **VeriCode** ("we", "us").

## The short version
- Lighter does not collect your data. We have no servers, no accounts and no analytics.
- Your photos are never uploaded, copied or sent anywhere by Lighter. All processing happens on your iPhone.
- We do not track you and we share nothing with third parties.

## What the app accesses
**Your photo library.** Lighter asks for permission to your Photos so it can show your photos and videos, let you mark them to keep or delete, and delete the ones you confirm. You can choose full access or a limited selection, and you can change this at any time in iOS Settings > Photos > Lighter. Lighter only reads photos to display them and to analyse them on your device, and only deletes after you confirm in the standard iOS prompt.

**iCloud photos.** If a photo is stored in iCloud Photos and not on your iPhone, iOS may download it from iCloud so Lighter can show it to you. This is done by Apple's Photos service under your Apple Account. Lighter's own analysis never downloads from iCloud: it uses only copies already on your iPhone and skips the rest. Lighter does not send any photo to us or to anyone else.

## What the app stores on your device
All of the following stays on your iPhone and is removed when you delete the app:
- **Daily deletion count and date**, to apply the free plan's daily limit.
- **Rating-prompt state** (for example, how many albums you finished and when you last saw the prompt), so the app does not ask for a rating too often.
- **Clean-up scan results.** Quick clean-up analyses your photos (not videos or screenshots) on your iPhone using Apple's Vision framework to find duplicates and pages and things. For each photo it saves the photo's Photos-library identifier, its dates, whether it appears to show people, whether it looks like a page or note, a quality score, and two numeric fingerprints (a small hash and a Vision feature print). These cannot be turned back into the picture. They are saved in the app's private storage on your iPhone so the scan does not repeat, and we never receive them. Like other app data, they may be included in your iPhone backups (iCloud or computer), which Apple handles under your settings and Apple Account.
- **Photos you have marked** during a session are held only while you review them.

## Purchases
Lighter offers an optional one-time in-app purchase, Lighter Pro. Payments are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details. The app checks your purchase status with Apple's StoreKit so it knows whether Pro is unlocked, and "Restore Purchases" asks Apple to confirm a previous purchase. Apple's own privacy policy governs that processing.

## Data we collect, share or sell
None. We do not collect personal data, we do not use advertising or analytics, we do not include third-party SDKs, and we do not sell or share any data. The app does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Children
Lighter is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Your choices and rights
Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase. You control everything through iOS: revoke photo access in Settings, and delete the app to remove all data it stored. Photos you delete go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days, where you can restore them. With iCloud Photos turned on, deletions also apply to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account.

## Changes
If this policy changes we will update the date above and publish the new version in the app and at the same web address. Material changes will also be noted in the App Store listing or the app.
