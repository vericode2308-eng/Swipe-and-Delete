Lighter for iPhone by VeriCode

Email support@vericodestudio.com for help, bug reports, privacy requests or questions about these pages. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what you were doing, and we will look into it. Please do not email photos.

Common questions

Lighter cannot see my photos

Open iOS Settings, then Photos, then Lighter, and choose Full Access or Limited Access. In Lighter, Settings, Photo Access opens that page.

I deleted a photo by mistake

Deleted photos move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can restore them for 30 days. Lighter only deletes after you confirm in the standard iOS prompt.

Space did not come back after deleting

Storage returns once Recently Deleted is emptied, either by you or automatically after 30 days.

Restoring a purchase

In Lighter, open Settings and choose Restore Purchases while signed in with the Apple Account that bought it.

Do you keep my photos or data?

No. Lighter has no account or server, and photos are never uploaded. Read the Privacy Policy for the full detail.
