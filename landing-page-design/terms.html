4. What the app does

Lighter lets you review the photos and videos in your Photos library and delete the ones you choose. Swipe left to mark a photo for deletion, swipe right to keep it. You then review the marked items and delete them with one iOS prompt.

It also offers a Quick clean-up that groups duplicates (identical or near-identical shots) and "Pages & Things" (documents, receipts and other utility or very low-quality photos), and a shortcut to the WhatsApp album if you have one. Groupings are suggestions made by automated analysis on your iPhone, and they can be wrong. You decide what to delete, and nothing is deleted automatically. Settings shows a running total of the space you have cleared.

The "room for about N more photos" figure on the Done screen is an estimate based on an average photo size. Space returns to your iPhone only after items leave Recently Deleted.
