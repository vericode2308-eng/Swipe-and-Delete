Pick an album, swipe, check, done

One gesture does all the work. Every screen below is the real app.

  1. 1

    Pick an album

    Recents, Screenshots, Videos, Selfies, Favourites or any album you made. Every album opens at its first photo.

  2. 2

    Swipe left or right

    Left marks a photo for deletion. Right keeps it. Photos you have passed stay in a ring beside the one in front, so one tap on Undo, or a drag along the dots, takes you back.

  3. 3

    Check before anything goes

    Tap Select to pick many photos at once by tapping or dragging across them. Review shows everything you marked, and a tap keeps one after all. Then iOS asks you once, and the photos move to Recently Deleted for 30 days.

  4. 4

    See the space you cleared

    A short celebration counts up the space you freed and how many photos you reviewed, kept and deleted.

Good questions, plain answers.

Does Lighter ever delete a photo without asking?

No. You swipe every photo yourself, the Review screen shows everything you marked, and iOS asks you once before anything is deleted. Quick clean-up only finds photos; you still swipe each one.

Can I get a deleted photo back?

Yes. Deleted photos go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and stay there for 30 days. With iCloud Photos on, they are removed from your other devices too.

Do my photos leave my iPhone?

No. Lighter never uploads anything. There is no account, no server and no analytics. The clean-up scan runs on the iPhone using small local thumbnails.

Why didn't my free storage go up straight away?

iOS keeps deleted photos in Recently Deleted for 30 days. The space comes back once that album is emptied, either by you or automatically.

What does the free plan include?

Swiping, keeping, reviewing and Quick clean-up are unlimited. You can delete up to 50 photos a day, and the count resets at midnight.

How much is Lighter Pro?

A single $4.99 purchase, shown in your local currency. It removes the daily limit. There is no subscription, and Restore Purchases is always in Settings.

Does Lighter remember where I stopped?

No, on purpose. Every album starts from its first photo, so there is no hidden history of what you kept.

Which iPhones does it work on?

Any iPhone running iOS 18 or later. Lighter follows your light or dark appearance, supports VoiceOver and scales with large text.
