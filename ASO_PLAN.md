# Swipe & Delete — ASO Plan

Read this before touching metadata, screenshots, pricing, review prompts or
release. Never advertise a feature that is not built and verified.
Store text drafts will live in `store_drafts/`, one file per field.

## Name and search
- **App name (30):** "Swipe & Delete: Photo Cleaner" (29). Draft; the name
  "Swipe & Delete" is fixed (D-13), the suffix carries the search term.
- **Subtitle (30):** "Clean your camera roll calmly" (29). Draft.
- **Keyword ideas (100, comma-separated, no repeats of name words):**
  gallery, storage, free up space, declutter, tidy, screenshots, camera roll,
  clear, organise, album. To be counted and trimmed in `store_drafts/`.

## Positioning
- One gesture: left deletes, right keeps. Nothing to learn.
- Calm, beautiful glass design instead of alarm-style "your phone is full"
  cleaners.
- No subscription. Free with 50 deletions a day; Pro is a one-time $4.99.
- Private: photos never leave the iPhone; no account; no tracking.

## Pricing
- Free download; non-consumable `com.vericodestudio.swipeanddelete.pro` at
  the $4.99 tier (D-12).
